Project: Lok-Yatra – Explore Eastern India

Project Description:

Lok-Yatra is a tourism and cultural exploration platform designed to showcase the rich heritage, traditions, history, natural beauty, and hidden destinations of Eastern India. The platform aims to connect travellers with the diverse cultures and unexplored tourist attractions of states such as West Bengal, Odisha, Bihar, Jharkhand, and the northeastern region.

It provides travellers with information about popular tourist destinations, historical monuments, local festivals, traditional cuisines, cultural experiences, and offbeat locations. Lok-Yatra aims to promote local tourism, support small businesses and local communities, and encourage sustainable travel through digital innovation.

Key Features:
- Discover popular and hidden tourist destinations.
- Explore local culture, traditions, and festivals.
- Get information about historical and heritage sites.
- Discover authentic regional food and local experiences.
- Access travel guides and suggested itineraries.
- Promote local artisans, businesses, and tourism communities.

Objective:To make exploring Eastern India easier, more engaging, and culturally enriching while promoting regional tourism and preserving its cultural identity.
