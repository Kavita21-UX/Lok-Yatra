Hero Destination Spotlight

The Crown Jewel of Magadha: Rajgir (Rajagriha)

Nestled in a verdant valley enclosed by five majestic holy hills (Ratnagiri, Vipulagiri, Vaibhargiri, Songiri, and Udaygiri), Rajgir was ancient India's first imperial capital. Today, it unites 2,500-year-old Buddhist & Jain heritage with India's most thrilling eco-safari and glass skywalk.

Vishwa Shanti Stupa & Ropeway
📍 Nalanda District, Bihar ★ 4.9 (Top Rated Eco-Spiritual Site)

Where Serenity Meets Grandeur

Perched atop Ratnagiri Hill at 400 meters, the dazzling white marble World Peace Pagoda (Vishwa Shanti Stupa) was consecrated under the guidance of Buddhist monk Nichidatsu Fujii. It features four gilded statues of Lord Buddha representing his birth, enlightenment, first sermon, and parinirvana. Reaching the peak via India's oldest scenic single-seater ropeway or modern 8-seater cabin ropeway is an unforgettable experience.

The peaceful Bamboo Grove gifted by King Bimbisara to Lord Buddha as his first monastery. Nearby lies the ancient stone jail from where the king gazed at Buddha atop Gridhakuta.

A staggering 40-kilometer ancient stone rampart built before the 6th century BCE without mortar. One of the oldest surviving masonry works in all of South Asia.

Curated Travel Itineraries

Whether you have a 3-day weekend or a full week, our step-by-step crafted routes help you experience the very best of Rajgir and Bihar effortlessly.

3-Day Rajgir & Nalanda Golden Weekend

Ideal for weekend travelers, families, and history enthusiasts seeking adventure and peace.

⏳ Duration: 3 Days / 2 Nights 🚆 Base: Rajgir / Patna ⚡ Pace: Comfortable
Day 1

Arrival in Rajgir & The Sacred Hills

Check into your Rajgir hotel or resort. In the morning, take the scenic ropeway to Ratnagiri Hill and visit the glowing white Vishwa Shanti Stupa. Hike to the nearby Gridhakuta (Vulture Peak) where Lord Buddha delivered sublime sermons. Enjoy a peaceful stroll through Venu Vana bamboo garden, visit Bimbisara's ancient stone jail, and end your evening with a rejuvenating dip in the Brahmakund hot sulfur springs.

Food Tip: Try authentic hot Silao ka Khaja from local bakeries right on the Rajgir-Nalanda road.
Day 2

Rajgir Glass Bridge, Nature Safari & Ghora Katora

Start your morning early at the Rajgir Nature Safari. Walk the thrilling Glass Skywalk suspended 250 feet over the lush green valley, try the zip-line, and embark on the zoo safari. In the afternoon, head to the eco-friendly Ghora Katora Lake on an electric buggy, enjoy tranquil boating around the colossal 70-foot pink sandstone Buddha statue surrounded by five hills.

Travel Tip: Book your Glass Bridge tickets online in advance via the Bihar Forest Department portal to avoid peak counter queues.
Day 3

Nalanda UNESCO World Heritage & Pawapuri Lotus Temple

Drive 15 km to Nalanda Mahavihara ruins. Spend the morning admiring the ancient stupas, lecture halls, and dormitories where scholars from across Asia studied. Visit the Xuanzang Memorial Hall and the Archaeological Museum. In the afternoon, proceed to Pawapuri (19 km away) to witness the pristine marble Jal Mandir floating on a lotus-covered lake where Lord Mahavira attained Nirvana.

Departure: Board evening trains from Rajgir or Patna Junction.

5-Day Sacred Enlightenment Circuit

Patna • Vaishali • Nalanda • Rajgir • Bodh Gaya

⏳ Duration: 5 Days / 4 Nights ✈️ Entry: Patna | Exit: Gaya 🧘 Spiritual & UNESCO
Day 1

Patna City Heritage & River Ganga

Arrive in Patna. Explore the historic Golghar with spiral stairs for panoramic Ganga views. Visit the world-class Bihar Museum, Sabhyata Dwar, and the sacred Takht Sri Patna Sahib Gurudwara in Old Patna. Enjoy the evening Ganga Aarti at Gandhi Ghat.

Day 2

Vaishali – The World's First Republic

Excursion across the Mahatma Gandhi Setu to Vaishali. Visit the ancient Ashokan Lion Pillar, Buddha's Relic Stupa, Kundalpur (birthplace of Lord Mahavira), and the peaceful Coronation Tank (Abhishek Pushkarni). Return to Patna in the evening.

Day 3

Nalanda Mahavihara & Pawapuri Jal Mandir

Drive to Nalanda. Marvel at the red-brick monasteries and Temple No. 3. Visit the Xuanzang Memorial Hall. Continue to Pawapuri for a peaceful visit to Jal Mandir, then arrive in Rajgir for evening hot spring relaxation.

Day 4

Rajgir Hills, Ropeway & Glass Skywalk

Ascend Ratnagiri Hill by ropeway to Vishwa Shanti Stupa and Vulture Peak. Experience the thrilling Rajgir Glass Bridge at the Nature Safari. Enjoy late afternoon boating at the serene Ghora Katora Lake before driving to Bodh Gaya.

Day 5

Bodh Gaya – The Peak of Enlightenment

Spend a meditative morning at the Mahabodhi Temple complex under the sacred Bodhi Tree. Visit the 80-foot Great Buddha statue and international monasteries (Thai, Japanese, Tibetan, Bhutanese). Depart from Gaya International Airport.

7-Day Grand Eastern India Explorer

From the Afghan Mausoleums of Sasaram to Rajgir, Nalanda, Bodh Gaya & Kolkata

⏳ Duration: 7 Days / 6 Nights 🚆 Multi-State Train & Road 🏰 Comprehensive Grand Tour
Day 1-2

Sasaram & Rohtas Cascades

Explore the stunning water tomb of Emperor Sher Shah Suri in Sasaram. Day-trip into the rugged Kaimur hills to witness the breathtaking Rohtasgarh Fort, Telhar Kund waterfall, and Tutla Bhawani canyon bridge.

Day 3-4

Bodh Gaya & Gaya Pind Daan Shrines

Experience the UNESCO Mahabodhi Temple, the sacred Falgu river ghats, Vishnupad Temple in Gaya, Dungeshwari cave temples, and international monastery architecture.

Day 5

Rajgir & Nalanda Wonder Loop

Vishwa Shanti Stupa, Ropeway, Glass Skywalk, Ghora Katora lake, and the majestic UNESCO ruins of Nalanda Mahavihara.

Day 6-7

Patna to Kolkata Grand Finale

Visit Patna Sahib and Bihar Museum. Board the express train to Kolkata. Enjoy an evening cruise along the Hooghly river by Howrah Bridge, Victoria Memorial, and savor Bengali rasgulla & street food.

Living Traditions & Flavors

The Flavors, Crafts & Heritage of Bihar

Indulge in time-honored culinary delicacies and take home legendary GI-tagged handicrafts crafted by generations of master artisans.

🥟
GI Tagged • Rajgir Special

Silao ka Khaja

Originating in the historic town of Silao situated right between Rajgir and Nalanda, this crisp, 52-layered sweet pastry is made from wheat flour, ghee, and cardamom sugar syrup. Legend holds that Lord Buddha was offered Khaja on his travels.

🍲
Culinary Icon of Bihar

Litti Chokha with Desi Ghee

Whole wheat dough balls stuffed with spiced roasted gram flour (sattu), ajwain, kalonji, and mustard oil, slowly baked over cow-dung or charcoal embers. Served drowned in melted pure desi ghee with spicy smoked eggplant and tomato chokha.

🍯
Winter Delicacy

Gaya Tilkut & Makhana Kheer

Gaya is world-famous for its feather-light pounded sesame seed brittle (Tilkut) prepared during Makar Sankranti. Pair it with creamy kheer made from Mithila Foxnuts (Makhana), of which Bihar produces 90% of the world supply.

🎨
GI Tagged Handcraft

Madhubani (Mithila) Folk Art

Practiced across North Bihar since the era of the Ramayana. Painted using twigs, nib-pens, and matchsticks with natural dyes made from indigo, turmeric, and flowers. Features geometric motifs of nature, deities, and courtly celebrations.

🧵
GI Tagged Silk

Bhagalpuri Tussar Silk

Known globally as the "Silk City", Bhagalpur's handloom weavers produce rich golden Tussar silk saris and stoles prized for their breathable texture, natural sheen, and centuries-old indigenous weaving techniques.

📜
Ancient Angika Heritage

Manjusha Art & Sujani Quilting

Manjusha is an ancient narrative scroll art based on the folklore of Bihula-Bishahari with signature pink, green, and yellow hues. Sujani is a delicate traditional quilting embroidery that tells women's social stories in stitch.
